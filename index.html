Eat a Food

Every 1000 points earns a food. Luck starts at +100%, and every 10 food types adds another +50%. Walk into the glowing circle to start a run. The gold stand sells potions, and the purple stand crafts Goldens, Rainbows, and Shadows.

Multiplayer

Create a room, or join with a code. Friends use this same address.

    Inventory

    Equip a food before a run. Points, growth, and speed are listed on each card.

    Shop

    Potions cost 1000 diamonds. Each one adds 10 minutes. Luck strength stacks up to ×5; other potions keep one strength and only stack time.

    Craft

    Combine 50 of a food into 1 Golden, 50 Goldens into 1 Rainbow, then 50 Rainbows into 1 Shadow. Each step is +2 points, 1.5× growth, and 1.5× speed.
